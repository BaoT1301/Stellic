Software Engineer II — Backend & Distributed Systems
Northlake Systems, Inc. — Reston, VA (hybrid, 3 days on-site)
Full-time · Entry to mid-level (0-2 years) · Requisition BE-2284

About the team
Northlake's Platform Infrastructure group owns the ingest, storage, and query
layer behind our logistics analytics product. We move roughly 40 billion events a
day off a fleet of customer edge devices, land them in a partitioned store, and
serve them back under a 200 ms p99 read budget. You would be the third engineer
on the ingest pod.

What you will do
- Design, build, and operate backend services in Go and Java that run as
  replicated, horizontally scaled deployments across three regions.
- Extend our event pipeline: partitioning, consumer group rebalancing, at-least-
  once delivery, idempotent writes, and backpressure handling under load spikes.
- Model and evolve relational schemas in PostgreSQL. You will write a lot of SQL
  and you will be expected to read a query plan and explain why an index is or is
  not being used.
- Own the correctness of concurrent code: locking strategy, race conditions,
  thread pools, and the failure modes that only show up when a node dies
  mid-write.
- Participate in design review. We write short design documents before we write
  code, and every engineer is expected to critique other people's designs.
- Instrument what you ship. Dashboards, structured logs, and alert thresholds
  are part of the deliverable.

Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related
  field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Working knowledge of data structures and algorithm analysis. You should be able
  to reason about the time and space cost of your own code and justify a choice
  between two data structures.
- Demonstrated understanding of operating systems fundamentals: processes and
  threads, virtual memory, scheduling, and file I/O.
- Experience writing concurrent or parallel programs, and an understanding of
  what makes distributed systems hard: partial failure, replication lag,
  consistency models, and consensus.
- Proficiency with SQL and relational database design, including normalization,
  transactions, and isolation levels.
- Familiarity with software design practice: version control, code review,
  automated testing, and incremental delivery.

Preferred qualifications
- Coursework or project experience in computer systems or computer architecture.
- Exposure to containerized deployment and infrastructure-as-code.
- Prior internship building a service that other people depended on.

Northlake Systems is an equal opportunity employer.
